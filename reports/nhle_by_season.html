NHLe by league, 2010-11 to 2025-26

NHL points per league point, season by season, from the NHLe network. Era-adjusted restates every season in 2025-26 NHL scoring (× the NHL's points per team-game ratio); Same season is the raw factor in that season's own NHL.

CHL = OHL, WHL and QMJHL combined (geometric mean weighted by crossover players). Hollow points: from 2025-26 the NCAA and CHL factors include a step for the rule change letting CHL players keep NCAA eligibility — one season of data so far, so treat them as provisional.
